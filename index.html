LLI Grammar Mastery

Six levels of grammar, from first sentences to full control. Each level has a Grammar Mastery Checklist, with one grammar point per teaching session. Interactive Quick Checks let teachers test every point in class and decide where each student is.

STAGE 1IntroducedTaught and understood with support.
STAGE 2PractisingUsed in guided tasks; errors still happen.
STAGE 3MasteredUsed independently in real speaking or writing, about 80%+ accurate.

Completing a checklist shows grammatical control broadly aligned with that CEFR level. It does not by itself certify overall CEFR proficiency.
